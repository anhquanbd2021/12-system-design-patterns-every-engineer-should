The breaker decides the blast radius.

A storefront calls a recommendations service through a 12-slot worker pool. Flip the dependency to dead and watch calls park on sockets that will never answer — then arm the breaker and replay the same kill.

Educational model. A deterministic tick simulation running entirely in your browser — no real services, no real traffic, no wall clock.
